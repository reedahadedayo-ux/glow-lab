For acne-prone, oily skin beginners: AM = Gentle cleanser (CeraVe Foaming) + Niacinamide + Sunscreen (Eucerin Oil Control). PM = Cleanser + BHA exfoliant 2-3x/week (Paula’s Choice) + Lightweight moisturizer. Always patch test and introduce one product at a time. Ask Lumi for a personalized plan.
Consistency + sun protection is key. Ingredients like Vitamin C (Garnier), Niacinamide (The Ordinary), and gentle exfoliation (BHA) can help visibly brighten over 4-8 weeks. Sunscreen daily (BoJ Relief Sun or LRP Anthelios) is non-negotiable to prevent further darkening. Lumi can build a routine around your budget.
Yes — everyone needs sunscreen. Melanin provides some natural protection but not enough. Sunscreen helps prevent hyperpigmentation, premature aging and skin damage. Our sunscreens like Beauty of Joseon Relief Sun have zero white cast on deep tones.
Use Vitamin C in the morning after cleansing, before moisturizer and sunscreen. 3-4 drops is enough. Store in cool, dark place. If you’re new, start with Garnier Vitamin C Serum (gentler) and always follow with SPF50+ in the day. Avoid mixing with retinol or strong AHAs in the same routine unless advised.
Retinol is powerful for texture, acne and fine lines but can irritate beginners. Start slowly: 1-2x/week at night, pea-sized amount, moisturize well, and use sunscreen daily (retinol increases sun sensitivity). If you have sensitive skin, Centella or Niacinamide may be better first steps. Ask Lumi or a dermatologist before starting high-strength retinol.
