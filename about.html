Our Story • Lagos, Nigeria

Skincare shouldn’t be confusing.

GlowLab was created to help Nigerians build simple, effective routines with trusted products and honest guidance — no gatekeeping, no overwhelm.

Why we started GlowLab

We kept seeing the same problems: fake products flooding the market, 12-step routines that don’t fit our climate, and beginners not knowing where to start for acne, hyperpigmentation, or sensitive skin.

GlowLab curates high-quality products from globally trusted brands — CeraVe, Cetaphil, La Roche-Posay, Beauty of Joseon, COSRX, The Ordinary, Skin1004, Eucerin, Neutrogena, Paula’s Choice, Garnier, Vichy — and makes them easily accessible across Nigeria.

Our mission is simple: help you build a routine you’ll actually stick to. Cleanser, treatment, moisturizer, sunscreen. Personalized by skin type, concern and budget.
