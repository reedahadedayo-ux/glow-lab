Our approach • GlowLab

Skincare shouldn’t be confusing.

A simpler way to explore skincare, with clear catalogue prices and room to ask questions. This is a demonstration, not a clinical service or an authenticity certification.

Why we started GlowLab

Skincare shopping can feel complicated. GlowLab brings the collection, prices and a simple shopping journey together, so you can explore at your own pace.

This demonstration preserves the original GlowLab collection, including its product names, images and naira prices. It does not certify authenticity, ingredients or clinical benefits, and no products are dispatched through this checkout.

Our mission is simple: help you build a routine you’ll actually stick to. Cleanser, treatment, moisturizer, sunscreen. Personalized by skin type, concern and budget.

A calmer place to start

Clear prices. Room to ask questions. A few everyday essentials. Skincare should feel approachable, without pressure to build an elaborate routine.

Our Values

What we stand for

Authenticity Over Hype

We favour clear information over hype. This demo catalogue is not an authenticity or medical certification.

Education First

No complicated routines for their own sake. Start with the basics, check packaging, and ask questions when you need help.

Skin Types Matter

Everyone starts somewhere. Explore the catalogue’s skin-type labels as shopping guidance, not a diagnosis or a guarantee of suitability. Check packaging and seek professional advice when needed.

Built for Nigeria

Prices in naira, familiar brands, and a simple shopping experience. This demo has no real charges or deliveries.

Meet Lumi — Your AI Skincare Advisor

Lumi is an AI shopping companion powered by Gemini through a secure backend. It uses catalogue and policy tools to answer questions, and asks you to approve products and support tickets.
