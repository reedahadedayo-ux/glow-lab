A little more clarity

Skincare shopping can feel complicated. GlowLab brings the collection, prices and a simple shopping journey together, so you can explore at your own pace.

Compare products by brand, category and price, then take your time deciding. Where detailed product information is unavailable, we say so—your packaging is the place to check ingredients and directions.

Our Values

What we stand for

Clarity over hype

Know what you’re choosing. We separate available product information from details you’ll need to check on the packaging.

Education First

No complicated routines for their own sake. Start with the basics, check packaging, and ask questions when you need help.

Skin Types Matter

Everyone starts somewhere. Explore the catalogue’s skin-type labels as shopping guidance, not a diagnosis or a guarantee of suitability. Check packaging and seek professional advice when needed.

Prices in naira

Browse familiar skincare brands with prices in Nigerian naira, so it’s easier to compare your options.
